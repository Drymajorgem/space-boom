GALACTIC HUNTER

SCORE: 0

Controls

Use W, A, S, D keys to fly. Aim with your Mouse. Click/Hold to shoot.

Homing Enemies

Asteroids track your position and fly straight toward you. Do not let them touch you!

Tip

Since you can move anywhere, use the screen space to dodge around incoming target vectors.
